# Chess Piece Vectors — License & Attribution

The vector Staunton chess piece designs in LAN Chess are derived from the iconic vector chess set created by **Colin M. L. Burnett (Cburnett)**.

- **Author**: Colin M. L. Burnett
- **Original Source**: Wikimedia Commons ([Chess Pieces in SVG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces))
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0) and GNU General Public License (GPL) version 2 or later.
- **Modifications for LAN Chess**:
  - Precision SVG path alignment in clean 45x45 viewBox.
  - Tailored color rendering: warm ivory fill (`#fdfbf7`) with dark outline for white pieces; deep charcoal fill (`#262629`) with crisp contrasting internal detail and subtle outline for black pieces.
  - Accessible sizing and pointer event pass-through (`pointer-events: none`) for chessboard interactions.
  - Bundled locally with zero runtime network/CDN dependencies.
